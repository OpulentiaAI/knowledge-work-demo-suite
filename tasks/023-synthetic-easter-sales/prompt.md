# Assess Easter Sales Collapse and Response

## Assignment

You are the performance analyst for Store 18 — Memphis. The store recorded an unusually weak Sunday on March 31. The district manager is considering a price promotion for the next holiday and asks whether the result reflects a demand, pricing, availability, or data-quality problem.

Using the files in `source_docs/`, prepare `easter_sales_incident_memo.md`. Compare March 31 with the prior Sunday, March 24.

Your memo must:

- quantify changes in payment net sales, transactions, units, basket value, discounts, and gross profit;
- identify the leading evidence-supported explanation and explicitly rule out at least two weaker explanations;
- use the chain and retail-calendar data appropriately, without treating calendar correlation as proof beyond the packet;
- state whether the price-promotion proposal is justified; and
- propose an operational response and a validation check for the next comparable holiday.

Use dollars and percentages. Do not describe a partial-sales day as a complete closure or data loss unless the data supports that conclusion.
