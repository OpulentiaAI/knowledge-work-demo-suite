# Diagnose Valentine Sales Growth and Mix

## Assignment

You are the performance analyst for Store 09 — Chicago. Leadership wants to know whether the February 13–14 sales gain was driven by a promotion, by more shoppers, or by a higher-value basket—and whether it is worth repeating.

Using the files in `source_docs/`, prepare `valentine_sales_diagnostic.md`. Compare February 13–14 with February 6–7, the same weekdays one week earlier.

Your diagnostic must:

- quantify changes in payment net sales, transactions, units, basket value, discount rate, and gross profit;
- identify the most material category-mix changes and connect them to the retail calendar only to the extent supported by evidence;
- state whether the evidence supports a promotion-led explanation;
- recommend a targeted next-event merchandising or inventory action and a measurement plan.

Use dollars and percentages. Clearly label observed findings and any inference.
