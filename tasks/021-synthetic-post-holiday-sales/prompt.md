# Diagnose Post-Holiday Sales Weakness

## Assignment

You are the regional retail performance analyst for Store 04 — San Antonio. The store manager believes that the January 2–6 sales decline indicates a local merchandising problem and wants an immediate discount response.

Using the files in `source_docs/`, prepare `post_holiday_sales_diagnostic.md` for the regional manager. Compare January 2–6 with the same weekdays in January 9–13, and use the chain data to distinguish a local issue from a broader pattern.

Your diagnostic must:

- quantify the change in sales after refunds, transactions, units, basket value, discount rate, gross profit, and refunds;
- rank the likely drivers, separating observed evidence from hypotheses;
- explain whether the proposed discount response is supported by the evidence;
- recommend two immediate actions and one measurement that would determine whether the decline persists after the post-holiday period.

Use dollars and percentages. Do not claim a cause that the packet does not support.
