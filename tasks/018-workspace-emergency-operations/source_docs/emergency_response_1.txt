# safeguards

The company's emergency protection includes four major categories of measures, the specific contents are as follows:

## 1. Communication and information security
### (1) Contact information storage
- Please see the attachment for details of the company’s emergency communications;
- Please see the attachment for details of correspondence between government departments and relevant units;
- Please see the attachment for details on surrounding enterprise communications.

### (2) Communication information update requirements (key points)
1. Company employee communication information is updated in a timely manner by the security officer;
2. The security officer is responsible for regularly reviewing and updating the communication information of government departments and relevant units;
3. The communication information of surrounding enterprises is also regularly reviewed and updated by the security officer;
4. The updated communication information will be communicated to all relevant departments within 24 hours by each responsible department, and the corresponding emergency plan attachment will be updated by the security officer.

## 2. Emergency team protection (key points)
The company has established an emergency response team consisting of five emergency response teams. The specific composition is as follows:
1. Emergency rescue team
2. Pollution Control Group
3. Alert and evacuation team
4. Medical emergency team
5. Logistics support team

Please see the attachment for the specific personnel list.

## 3. Emergency supplies and equipment support
1. The company is equipped with complete emergency supplies, which can be deployed and used during emergency response; please see the attachment for a list of emergency supplies;
2. The company has also signed an emergency mutual aid agreement with surrounding companies, which can make full use of the existing emergency resources of surrounding companies to provide guarantee for emergency work.

## 4. Other guarantees (including financial guarantee)
### Funding guarantee:
1. In accordance with the provisions of the Production Safety Law, ensure necessary special funds for emergency rescue. Emergency rescue funds are included in safety fund investment.
2. Scope of use of emergency rescue funds:
   - Supply and procurement of emergency supplies, equipment and facilities;
   - Training and drills for emergency rescue teams;
   - Various expenses incurred during the accident emergency response;
   - Used to reward those who have performed meritorious services in emergency rescue and emergency response after the accident rescue.
